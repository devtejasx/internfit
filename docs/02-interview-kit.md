# Student Interview Kit (5–8 students, 20 min each)

**Status: none conducted yet.** Fill in `evidence/interviews/` only with real, consented sessions. Never paraphrase into quotes.

## Who to recruit
- Currently enrolled UG students (2nd–4th year), from Tier 2/3 colleges. Prefer a mix of CS/IT and non-CS (commerce, pharma, mechanical, design).
- Has applied to at least one internship or job in the last 3 months, on any platform.
- Aim for at least 3 who have used Beep, and some who haven't.
- **Channels:** your own college or batch WhatsApp groups, juniors, TPO/placement coordinator, LinkedIn connections from Tier 2/3 colleges. Use only channels you are already part of. No cold scraping.

## Consent (read aloud, record "yes")
> "I'm a student doing a product research assignment. I'd like to ask about your recent internship or job search. It takes about 20 minutes. I'll take notes but won't record your name or college in anything I share. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is that OK?"

## Script (open-ended, non-leading)
**Warm-up**
1. What year and branch are you in? What kind of role are you hoping for next?

**Most recent experience (spend most of the time here)**
2. Tell me about the last internship or job you applied for. Walk me through it from when you found it to now.
3. How did you decide that role was worth applying to?
4. What did you send? How did you prepare it? How long did that take?
5. What happened after you applied?
6. If you heard nothing back, what did you think the reason was? What did you do next?

**Tools and workarounds**
7. Which apps, websites, people or groups did you use during that process? What did each one actually help with?
8. Have you ever paid for anything to help with your career search (a course, certificate, premium plan)? How did that go?
9. Was there a point where you got stuck or gave up? What happened?

**Priorities (ask only after the open questions)**
10. Of everything you've told me, what was the most frustrating part? Why?
11. If that part were fixed, what would be different for you?

**Close**
12. Is there anything about looking for internships I should have asked but didn't?

*Avoid:* "Would an AI fit checker help you?" and "Isn't it annoying that recruiters don't reply?" Those are leading questions. Show the prototype only **after** the interview, as a separate usability task.
